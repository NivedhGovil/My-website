Books to Recommend

The books I would hand to someone else, and the reason I would hand them over.

Reading is where most of my writing starts. These are the books that have stayed with me — not a list of everything I have read, but the ones I keep recommending to people who ask.

  • The Lightning Thief

    Rick Riordan · Percy Jackson and the Olympians, Book 1

    The one that started it for me. Putting mythology into the real world with a touch of fiction is a genius idea, and Percy — the son of Poseidon — is my favourite character in anything I have read. If you have never read Riordan, start here.

  • The Last Olympian

    Rick Riordan · Percy Jackson and the Olympians, Book 5

    My all-time favourite. After the first four books I could not wait to find out whether Olympus would be saved or destroyed, and the ending earns everything the series spent four books building.

  • The House of Hades

    Rick Riordan · The Heroes of Olympus, Book 4

    Percy and Annabeth's journey through Tartarus is engrossing — and terrifying. It is also full of friendship and courage, which is what makes the terrifying part bearable.

  • The Heroes of Olympus

    Rick Riordan · The whole series

    My favourite series. The blend of Greek and Roman mythology is superb, and it is worth reading the five books in order rather than picking one out of the middle.

Next on the list

The Wrath of the Triple Goddess by Rick Riordan — not read yet, but I am looking forward to it.
