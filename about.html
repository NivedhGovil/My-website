The longer version

The part people read after they have liked something you wrote.

Write this

Two or three paragraphs in your own voice. Useful things to cover: where you are from and where you live now, what you are curious about, how you got into writing and into building things, and what you are working on at the moment.

Aim for 120–200 words. Write it the way you would explain yourself to someone your own age, not the way a CV would.
