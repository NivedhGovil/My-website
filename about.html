Hardships

What went wrong

My hardware projects often crashed out. These are the questions I get asked about that, and what I actually did.

  1. You did not top your class one year. What did you do about it?

    I learned from my mistakes, I improved, and continued to work harder. That's my advice to all of you: if one mistake happens, learn from it and then move on.

  2. What do you do when a build refuses to work?

    I try to think what went wrong. If I get it, then I fix it, or sometimes I just rework and try to think what could work out.

  3. What is the hardest part of writing for you?

    Sometimes the hardest thing is just choosing what to write about, as there are lots of ideas and topics. Another thing I face difficulty with is using language techniques like assonance. I guess I need to improve my vocabulary and the writer's effect.
