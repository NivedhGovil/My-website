Blaming the Indians

A poem by Nivedh Govil

They say, “Spicy food? Must be Indian”.
What about Mexican and Korean?

Indians are deeply religious and are temple-lovers,
But don’t Christians visit the church every Sunday
and what about Muslims, who pray five times a day?

What did Indians contribute to the world?
Who invented the number zero?
Who made the first Covid Vaccine?

Cricket lovers are only Indian,
But so are the British and Australians.

Replace the ‘T’ with a ‘D’,
Congratulations, you mastered the Indian accent.
Say water as ‘wohaah’ or ‘watah’.

Another conflict, must be between India and Pakistan,
But it can also be between another pair of countries.

The entire world is too big too diverse,
To be trapped in boxes,
Don’t pick on others,
Or the same can happen to you.
