A funny incident in the park

A short story by Nivedh Govil

The Sun shone as bright as a blazing fire in the sky. As my friend and I walked through the park, we saw tall trees and flowers which had vivid colours like those in a painting. The birds were chirping their sweet melody in the sky and the calm breeze was refreshing. My friend was laughing loudly on the phone talking to his other friend which irked me because I wanted to talk to him too, about a lot of things. I got angry and started walking fast like the wind and soon left him behind, only to realize that the lost case had not even seen me walk ahead. I knew that he was terribly lost in his own world. Suddenly he dropped his phone and I laughed like a drain.
