A letter to my favourite author, Rick Riordan

A letter by Nivedh Govil · 6th January 2025

6th January 2025

Dear Rick Riordan,

I hope that this letter finds you in good health. You are my favorite author. I read your first book of the Percy Jackson series, The Lightning Thief, about an year ago. I love the way you write your books. It is a genius idea to add mythology in the real world with a touch of fiction. Your books are lengthy, interesting and adventurous revolving around the central character, Percy, the son of the mighty god of the sea, Poseidon. He is my favorite character. I have read almost all of your books. The Heroes of Olympus series is my favorite. A blend of Greek and Roman mythology is superb! My two favorite books are-

  • The House of Hades: Percy and Annabeth’s journey in Tartarus is engrossing – and terrifying! It is also full of friendship and courage.
  • The Last Olympian: The last book of the series was my all-time favorite. After reading the first 4 books, I was very excited to read the ending and whether Olympus would be saved or destroyed.

I am looking forward to reading The Wrath of The Triple Goddess.

Your fan,
Nivedh Govil
