A talking dog?

A short story by Nivedh Govil

I was solving a math problem in my room, when suddenly my chihuahua, Niel came and rubbed his nose on my leg.

“Feeling bored right?”, I asked. My parents were shopping in a mall and left me at home.

“Yup”, said my dog.

“What! You can speak? In English?”, I exclaimed, my mouth open in shock.

“Of course! I was sent from Doggyland, to learn things from the human world.”, Neil replied.

I fainted.

I was brought back to consciousness a minute later.

“Thank God, the CPR worked. It was the first time I performed it.”, Neil aka Dog Spy said.

I fainted.

He brought me back to consciousness again.

“You are so cool!”, I said.

“I am. I can also fly.”, admitted Neil, before performing a super weird pose. Elegant wings grew from his back and within seconds, he was mid-air.

“Woah”, I exclaimed for the second time.

“Can you grow bigger and give me a ride?”, I asked curiously.
