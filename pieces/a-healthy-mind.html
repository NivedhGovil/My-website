A healthy mind is the foundation of a meaningful life

By Nivedh Govil

Imagine waking up every morning to a storm you cannot escape. Or carrying a backpack that gets heavier every day.

Well, the backpack is your brain, and its load is your mental health.

But what is mental health? Mental health is our psychological, emotional and social well-being. It is a crucial part of our development as it influences every aspect of your life, from decision-making to stress management.

One can protect mental health by managing stress, getting enough sleep, talking openly, exercising regularly, and maintaining positive, supportive relationships.

To spread awareness about mental health, the 10th of October is celebrated as World Mental Health Day around the world.

Habit formation is another way of keeping your mind healthy. It is when you convert your repeated actions into automatic behaviors. For example, when you wake up in the morning, instead of your parents reminding you to brush daily, make it a habit and next time you will have done it before your parents tell you to do so.

Good habits improve productivity, health, and mental well-being, and help in reducing stress. We can build habits by starting small, setting clear goals, staying consistent, and making rewarding progress. Over time, they create discipline and make positive behaviors automatic, leading to lasting success and stability.

A healthy mind isn’t found—it’s built, one habit at a time.
