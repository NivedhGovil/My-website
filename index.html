Nivedh Govil

Writer · Reader · Observer

Hello, and welcome. I am Nivedh Govil, and this is where I keep the things I write — the arguments I want to make, the images I cannot shake, and the people I invent to say what I cannot say plainly.

My writing tends to sit at the meeting point of curiosity and everyday life: articles when an idea needs to be thought through, poems when it needs to be felt, and short stories when it needs somebody to live it.
