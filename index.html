About the person behind the pages.

I am a student, and most of what appears here starts as ordinary curiosity — something I noticed, argued about, or could not stop turning over. Writing happens to be the way I pin it down.

New work is added to the sections above as it is finished, and new sections will appear as there is something to put in them. If something here stays with you, that is the best possible outcome.
