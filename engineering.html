Arduino Radar

An ultrasonic sensor mounted on a servo, sweeping back and forth and reporting how far away things are — the same idea as a radar, built out of two parts and a bit of trigonometry.

Built with
  • Arduino Uno
  • HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor
  • Servo motor
  • Jumper wires
What it does
  • Servo sweeps the sensor through an arc, a few degrees at a time
  • Ultrasonic sensor times an echo to measure distance at each angle
  • Angle and distance are plotted as a moving radar display
  • Objects show up as blips at the bearing where they were found

Arduino Crane

A working crane arm driven by servos — it lifts, swings and sets a load down again. The hard part is not the lifting; it is stopping the arm from shaking itself apart on the way.

Built with
  • Arduino Uno
  • Servo motors
  • Cardboard / frame
  • Thread and hook
  • Jumper wires
What it does
  • Servos drive the lift, the swing and the grab
  • The arm rotates to move a load from one place to another
  • Built from scratch rather than from a kit
  • The second video is a walkthrough of how it goes together

Tiranga on an RGB LED

One RGB LED, three colours, and the Indian flag — made for Independence Day. Mixing an exact saffron out of red, green and blue is fiddlier than it sounds.

Built with
  • Arduino Uno
  • RGB LED
  • Resistors
  • Jumper wires
What it does
  • A single RGB LED mixes each colour from red, green and blue channels
  • Cycles through saffron, white and green
  • Colour values are tuned by hand until the saffron looks right
  • Made for 15 August
